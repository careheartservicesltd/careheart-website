Our Story

Built with Heart,
Grown with Purpose

Care Heart Ltd. was born from a shared vision: that every person — regardless of ability, background, or circumstance — deserves quality care and a safe, clean environment.

🌱

April 2025 — Founded

Jeannette Rodríguez and Aixa Vásquez incorporated Care Heart Ltd. in Calgary, Alberta, combining their expertise in maintenance, cleaning, and human services.

🤝

Our Three Pillars

From day one, we built three service areas: property maintenance, cleaning services, and disability support — united by one purpose: genuine care.

🚀

Growing in Calgary

We are actively building our team and expanding our reach across Calgary, with a commitment to becoming the city's most trusted care services company.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What areas of Calgary do you serve?+
We serve Calgary and surrounding areas in Alberta. Contact us to confirm service availability in your specific neighborhood.
Are your disability support services covered by FSCD?+
We work with families receiving FSCD (Family Support for Children with Disabilities) funding. Contact us to discuss your specific situation and how we can support your family's needs.
Do you offer one-time or recurring services?+
We offer both! Whether you need a one-time deep clean, occasional maintenance, or ongoing weekly support services, we can create a schedule that works for you.
Are your staff trained and background-checked?+
Yes. All our team members are carefully screened, trained in ethical treatment and professional standards, and committed to the highest quality of care and service.
How do I get started?+
Simply fill out our contact form below or send us an email. We'll respond within 24 hours to discuss your needs and provide a personalized quote at no cost.
Do you provide services for commercial properties?+
Absolutely! We offer cleaning and maintenance services for residential, commercial, and industrial environments. Contact us for a custom commercial quote.
